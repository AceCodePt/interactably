Details, dialog & popover

Three revealable strategies in one row: a <details>, a modal <dialog> and a [popover]. Each is detected at connect and driven through the platform’s own open state — so focus trapping, the top layer, light dismiss and Escape come for free.

Why not just use hidden?

Because each platform element already exposes its own open state.

A native <dialog>, opened with showModal().
A popover in the top layer, toggled through showPopover().
