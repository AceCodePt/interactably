A secret you can reveal

attributable × modifiable × classable. The value is masked by type="password"; attributable swaps the attribute to reveal it, and classable marks the active button. Why two buttons? The grammar reads value and checked, not type, so a single toggle can’t branch on the current state — the two phrases are the honest branch.

HTML 
<input id="pw" implements="attributable" type="password" value="hunter2">

    <button implements="classable"
            on-click="#pw.setAttr({name: 'type', value: 'text'}); #show.add('active'); #hide.remove('active')">Show</button>
    <button implements="classable"
            on-click="#pw.setAttr({name: 'type', value: 'password'}); #hide.add('active'); #show.remove('active')">Hide</button>
