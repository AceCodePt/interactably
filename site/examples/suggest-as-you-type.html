Suggest as you type

requestable × focusable × revealable. A debounced send() fetches a fragment of options and swaps it into the listbox; on-response="#city-list.show()" reveals it. Press ↓ in the field to hop focus into the list, then arrow through — each option arrives already wired, because its own on-keydown / on-click phrases attach on insertion. Enter or a click picks the city into the field.

    Show the markup
    HTML 
    <input id="city" type="search" placeholder="Type a city, press ↓"
           implements="requestable modifiable focusable prevent-default"
           requestable-url="./fragments/cities.html"
           requestable-target="#city-list"
           on-input="#city.debounce(250).send()"
           on-response="#city-list.show()"
           on-keydown="arrowdown: #sug-1.focus(); escape: #city-list.show(false)">
    <ul id="city-list" implements="revealable" hidden></ul>
