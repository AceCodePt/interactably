Optimistic send, offline fallback

One on-click renders the message optimistically and then sends it in the same chain (#list.render({…}); #form.send()). When the network is down, the form fires request-error with the reserved offline key, and the keyed on-request-error="offline: #list.undo()" rolls the row back.

    Turn your network off and send: the row renders first, then undoes on the failure.
