Remember my theme

storable × attributable × revealable. One element owns the storage — #theme-store carries storable-id="theme", on-load="this.restore()" and the on-restore(name:string) handler. The three choice buttons target it by id and call save({name: …}) with their own value; the handler paints whatever data-theme it is handed onto the card and flashes the toast. Nothing is stored until a phrase calls save(), and Forget clears the owner. Reload the page to see it come back.

storable keeps the choice, attributable paints data-theme on this card, and revealable flashes the toast — the three-way intersection the card is built around.
