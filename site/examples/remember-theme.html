Remember my theme

storable × attributable × revealable. Three buttons declare a storable-value under one key; clicking saves the choice, and on-load="this.restore()" replays it: on-restore paints data-theme on the card and flashes the toast. Nothing is stored until a phrase calls save(), and Forget clears the key. Reload the page to see it come back.

storable keeps the choice, attributable paints data-theme on this card, and revealable flashes the toast — the three-way intersection the card is built around.
