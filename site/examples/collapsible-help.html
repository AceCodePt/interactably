A collapsible help panel

One button, one phrase: on-click="#panel.toggle()". revealable figures out the element on its own — it toggles a revealable-open attribute here, el.open on a <details> or <dialog>, popover state on a [popover]. The button’s aria-expanded stays in sync automatically.

This panel is a <section implements="revealable">. revealable reads the element: revealable-open here, open on a <details> or <dialog>, popover state on a [popover]. The trigger is on-click="#panel.toggle()", and the implementation keeps the button’s aria-expanded in sync.
