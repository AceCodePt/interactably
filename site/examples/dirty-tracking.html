Unsaved changes

dirtyable compares each field against its platform default — defaultValue for an input, defaultChecked for a checkbox — and fires dirty when you diverge and clean when you return. It writes nothing; the status line is a separate element the two events drive.

All saved — nothing has diverged from its default.

HTML 
<input id="name" implements="dirtyable attributable"
       on-dirty="#status.set('Unsaved changes…').setAttr({name: 'data-state', value: 'dirty'})"
       on-clean="#status.set('All saved.').removeAttr('data-state')">
<input id="remember" type="checkbox" implements="dirtyable attributable"
       on-dirty="#status.set('Unsaved changes…').setAttr({name: 'data-state', value: 'dirty'})"
       on-clean="#status.set('All saved.').removeAttr('data-state')">
<output id="status" implements="modifiable attributable" data-state="clean">All saved.</output>
