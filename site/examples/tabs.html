Tabs

Three buttons, three panels, no JavaScript. Each button shows one panel and hides the other two — a phrase is just ;-separated verbs. revealable toggles each panel’s hidden state.

Overview. A plain <section> whose visibility is driven by revealable. The other two panels stay hidden until you pick them.

Specs. One phrase, three verbs: #tab-specs.show(); #tab-overview.show(false); #tab-notes.show(false).

Notes. The verbs can be anything — set(), setAttr(), even a send() to fetch a tab’s content over the network.

Show the markup
HTML 
<div class="tabbar">
      <button
              on-click="#tab-overview.show(); #tab-specs.show(false); #tab-notes.show(false)">Overview</button>
      <button
              on-click="#tab-overview.show(false); #tab-specs.show(); #tab-notes.show(false)">Specs</button>
      <button
              on-click="#tab-overview.show(false); #tab-specs.show(false); #tab-notes.show()">Notes</button>
    </div>

    <section id="tab-overview" implements="revealable" class="tabpanel" revealable-open="true">
      <p>…</p>
    </section>
    <section id="tab-specs" implements="revealable" class="tabpanel" hidden>
      <p>…</p>
    </section>
    <section id="tab-notes" implements="revealable" class="tabpanel" hidden>
