Sign-up form with a guard

validate() checks the form and stops the chain when it’s invalid. && continues on completion, || continues only on a guard abort: setAttr runs when the form is valid, the alert shows when it is not. The field uses pattern rather than type="email", so the browser only complains when you submit, not while you type.

<form id="signup" novalidate
          implements="prevent-default validatable attributable"
          on-submit="#signup-alert.show(false); this.validate().setAttr({name: 'data-signed', value: 'true'}) || #signup-alert.show()">
      <label>Email
        <input name="email" type="text" pattern="[^@\s]+@[^@\s]+\.[^@\s]+" required placeholder="you@example.com">
      </label>
      <button type="submit">Sign up</button>
