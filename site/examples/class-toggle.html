Toggle one class, keep the rest

classable exposes classList through three verbs, one class name per call. Two decisions are fixed: there is no force argument on toggle() — add()/remove() are the forced forms — and the browser’s own rules decide what a name may be, so a multi-token string reaches classList whole and throws its own error.
