Revealable panel

This panel is a <section is="interactable-section" implements="revealable">. revealable derives its strategy from the element: data-open here, open on a <details> or <dialog>, popover state on a [popover]. The trigger is on-click="#panel.toggle()", and the implementation keeps the button’s aria-expanded in sync.
