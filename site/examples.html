Examples

A list of the working examples. Every one is a real UI built entirely from the HTML on its own page — open a page, click things, and hit Show the markup to copy the example straight into your own site. Several pages compose two or more implementations into one flow; each entry names the implementations involved.

Moving between these pages animates rather than flashing: the shared stylesheet opts every page into a cross-document view transition, so the header, main column and footer morph in place while the rest cross-fades. It is CSS-only and applies to same-origin navigations; a browser without support simply loads the next page as usual.

Single implementations

  • One button, one panel

    A trigger, a receiver, and a declaring script tag — the whole idea in a few lines.

    revealable
  • Price calculator

    Rows, quantities and a total recomputed from sum('#list .amount') after every change; on-keydown(code:`Escape`) resets a field.

    modifiable · dirtyable · attributable · renderable · formattable · prevent-default
  • A collapsible help panel

    toggle() figures out the element's own open state — details, dialog, popover or attribute.

    revealable
  • Live preview: debounce, keys & once

    Literal-matched on-keydown(code:`Enter`) / (code:`Escape`) declarations, a debounce, and a once() that spends itself on the first click.

    modifiable · dirtyable · attributable · prevent-default · revealable · on-keydown
  • Roving focus, written out

    Arrow keys, literal-matched on-keydown(code:`ArrowDown`), move real focus through a list; the ids are positional and the wrap is written out.

    focusable · prevent-default · on-keydown
  • Theme & attribute switcher

    setAttr, toggleAttr and removeAttr against a live swatch, plus a logger.

    attributable · logger
  • Toggle one class, keep the rest

    classList writes through three verbs, one class name per call.

    classable
  • A note that grows with you

    A textarea that grows with your text, marks edits as dirty, and cleans pastes.

    auto-grow · dirtyable · attributable · pastable · modifiable
  • A character counter

    length() counts in UTF-16 units — the same unit maxlength uses.

    attributable · modifiable
  • An age gate

    A guard verb: validate() passes the min=18 constraint and && then shows the consent panel.

    validatable · revealable
  • Details, dialog & popover

    Three revealable strategies in one row, each driven through the platform's own open state.

    revealable
  • A scroll spy, written out

    A current-section indicator follows the reader with enter and leave, a measured sticky-header margin, and a full-view read mark.

    attributable · on-intersect
  • Tabs

    Three buttons, three panels, no JavaScript.

    revealable
  • Three synchronized sections, written out

    Nine panels, three tab bars, one stored selection — replayed by one literal-matched restore site per value; the honest price of push.

    storable · revealable · on-restore
  • Sign-up form with a guard

    validate() stops the chain on an invalid submit; the || branch shows the alert.

    prevent-default · validatable · attributable · revealable
  • Click bubbling, made explicit

    Nested triggers bubble like nested onclick — until no-propagate stops one.

    logger · no-propagate
  • Order form — the asynchronous seam

    send() posts the form; on-response renders the body into the receipt — the asynchronous seam.

    prevent-default · requestable · renderable · revealable
  • Load a quote over the network

    A GET fragment that arrives with its own trigger inside, so the wiring is already live.

    requestable
  • Copy a snippet — the flash is delayable

    copy() copies a target's text and fires copy; the flash is setAttr, delay, removeAttr.

    copyable · attributable
  • A paste transform

    pasted fires after the paste has landed — whitespace collapses to single spaces, and only pastes are touched.

    pastable · modifiable
  • Unsaved changes, written out

    dirty and clean fire as fields diverge from and return to their platform defaults; a status line listens.

    dirtyable · attributable · modifiable
  • A list you can grow and shrink

    render stamps a template row with an author id, the × button deletes its own row by targeting that id, and undo removes the last stamped row.

    renderable
  • Format a number or date

    formattable hands the value to Intl.NumberFormat / Intl.DateTimeFormat with an options object.

    formattable · modifiable
  • Log from a phrase

    log() prints to the console from any phrase, naming the element and the trigger's source.

    logger

Compositions

  • A to-do list that survives reload

    A real GET seeds rows, then local add, delete, and undo keep the DOM mirrored in local storage; restore replays the stored markup on reload.

    requestable · renderable · storable · modifiable · on-restore
  • A shop with an anonymous cart

    Static products stamp one line each into a cart; the total and markup mirror survive reload, and every restored line can be removed.

    renderable · storable · modifiable · formattable · on-restore
  • A multi-step form, gated on validity

    A radio stepper opens only after each form becomes user-valid; breaking an earlier form closes every later step and explains why.

    validatable · revealable · attributable · modifiable · prevent-default
  • Suggest as you type

    A debounced send() renders options into a listbox; literal-matched on-keydown(code:`ArrowDown`) roves real focus through them.

    requestable · renderable · focusable · revealable · on-keydown
  • Validate, then send

    this.validate().send() || #alert.show() — the guard meets the async seam.

    validatable · requestable
  • Remember my theme

    A stored choice paints data-theme on the card and flashes a toast — from a single restore site, however many values it remembers.

    storable · attributable · revealable · on-restore
  • One tab bar, remembered

    The open tab persists and refocuses on reload; one restore site literal-matches the stored value — a three-way intersection.

    storable · revealable · focusable · on-restore
  • Open, and focus lands

    One chain does both: show() opens the dialog, focus() lands in the field.

    revealable · focusable
  • Only the characters you allow

    on-input rewrites the value to keep only the characters you allow.

    modifiable · pastable · dirtyable
  • Optimistic send, offline fallback

    render stamps the message, send fires in the same chain, and a fetch rejection fires request-offline so undo rolls the row back.

    renderable · requestable · prevent-default
  • A secret you can reveal

    Swap type between password and text — two buttons, because the grammar can't read type.

    attributable · classable
